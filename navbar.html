Meet Some of Our Animals

From the king of the savanna to the gentle giants of the forest — every resident has a story.

Simba the Lion

Panthera leo

The king of the savanna, known for his deep, resonant roar that echoes across the park at dawn.

Raja the Tiger

Panthera tigris

The largest cat species, Raja loves swimming in his pool and playing with giant enrichment balls.

Tembo the Elephant

Loxodonta africana

The largest land animal, Tembo is a gentle giant who creates abstract paintings with his trunk.

Kipenzi the Giraffe

Giraffa camelopardalis

The tallest resident at 16 feet, Kipenzi uses her 18-inch tongue to strip leaves from acacia trees.

10:00 AM – 11:30 AM · All ages
