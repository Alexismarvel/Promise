Conservation First

We believe zoos have a responsibility to protect wildlife. Every program, partnership, and habitat is built with conservation at its core.

Species Survival Plan

PromisesZOO participates in over 15 SSP breeding programs for endangered species, including black rhinos, western gorillas, orangutans, and polar bears. These carefully managed programs maintain genetic diversity and ensure viable populations for the future.

Field Conservation

We fund and partner with conservation projects across four continents. Our support helps protect critical habitats, combat poaching, and support local communities in coexisting with wildlife. In the past year alone, we contributed over $250,000 to field programs.

  • Rhino protection units in South Africa
  • Rainforest restoration in Borneo
  • Sea turtle nesting beach preservation in Costa Rica

Research & Veterinary Science

Our on-site veterinary team conducts groundbreaking research in reproductive biology, nutritional science, and wildlife medicine. We collaborate with universities worldwide to advance the science of animal care and conservation.

Education & Advocacy

Every visit to PromisesZOO is an opportunity to learn. Our education programs reach over 50,000 students annually through school visits, workshops, and outreach programs, inspiring the next generation of conservationists.

Support Our Mission

Every ticket, membership, and animal adoption directly funds our conservation work.
