Fun for All Ages

From toddlers to grandparents, there is something for everyone at PromisesZOO. Explore, learn, and play together.

🖐️

Interactive Animal Encounters

All ages · Included with admission

Get up close with gentle ambassador animals at our daily encounter stations. Pet a tortoise, marvel at a macaw, or watch a snake slither by — with expert keepers guiding every moment.

🎨

Wildlife Craft Corner

Ages 3–12 · Free

Let your little ones unleash their creativity at our craft station. Make animal masks, paint paw prints, and create nature-inspired art to take home as a souvenir.

🍦

Family Picnic & Play Areas

All ages · Free

Take a break at our shaded picnic grove with tables, benches, and a natural playground. Grab a snack from the Safari Cafe or bring your own lunch and enjoy the scenery.

📚

Keeper Talks & Feeding Shows

All ages · Daily schedule

Watch our animal care team in action during scheduled talks and feeding demonstrations. Learn how we train, enrich, and care for the animals — and ask your burning questions.

🚂

Zoo Express Train

All ages · $8/person

Hop on the Zoo Express for a relaxing ride through the park. The train stops at four stations, so you can explore each zone at your own pace.
